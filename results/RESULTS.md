## What the columns mean

- **Frames with a hand**: share of processed frames in which MediaPipe returned at least one hand. This is coverage, not accuracy.
- **Mean handedness score**: MediaPipe's own confidence in its left/right label, averaged over all detected hands.
- **`unknown` side**: frames where both hands received the same label. The lower-scoring hand is kept but marked `unknown` instead of being discarded.
- **Left-hand-left-of-right-hand**: in frames with both an `L` and an `R` hand, how often the `L` wrist is left of the `R` wrist in the image. This is a plausibility check for the left/right labels, not a ground-truth measurement.
- **Hand-free stretches**: runs of consecutive processed frames with no hand found.

## What this report does NOT measure

- Whether the 21 points sit on the correct joints. That needs hand-labelled frames, which are not made yet.
- Whether any single left/right label is correct. Only the plausibility check above is available.
- Identity stability, jitter or recovery after a hand re-enters the view. There is no tracking step yet.
